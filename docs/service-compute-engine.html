What it is

Infrastructure-as-a-Service VMs. Full control of the OS. Billed per second with sustained and committed use discounts.

Machine families

A3/G2GPU/accelerator for ML

Cost savers

  • Spot VMs - up to 60-91% off, preemptible.
  • Committed use discounts - 1 or 3 year.
  • Sustained use discounts - automatic.

Scaling & HA

Managed Instance Groups (MIGs) provide autoscaling, autohealing, rolling updates, and multi-zone spread. Pair with a load balancer.

🎯 Exam Tips

💡 Remember
  • Spot VMs can be reclaimed with 30s notice - only for fault-tolerant work.
  • MIGs (not unmanaged groups) give autoscaling and autohealing.
  • Sustained use discounts apply automatically; CUDs require commitment.
  • Use instance templates to define MIG VM configuration.
