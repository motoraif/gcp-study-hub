🌎 Spanner

Globally distributed, strongly consistent relational database.

What it is

A fully managed relational database that scales horizontally while providing strong consistency and up to 99.999% availability in multi-region configs. Unique in combining SQL, scale, and consistency.

Scaling & availability

  • Capacity in nodes or processing units.
  • Regional vs multi-region instance configs.
  • TrueTime underpins global strong consistency.

Schema design

Choose primary keys that avoid hotspots (don't use monotonically increasing keys); use interleaved tables for parent-child locality.

🎯 Exam Tips

💡 Remember
  • Spanner is the answer for global scale + strong consistency + high SLA.
  • Avoid monotonically increasing primary keys to prevent hotspots.
  • Multi-region configs raise availability to 99.999%.
  • Use it for relational OLTP at scale, not for ad-hoc analytics (that is BigQuery).
