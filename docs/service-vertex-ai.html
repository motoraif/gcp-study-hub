What it is

Google Cloud's unified ML platform. Covers data prep, training (AutoML or custom), the Model Registry, serving via Endpoints, Pipelines for MLOps, and Model Garden/Gemini for generative AI.

Training & serving

  • AutoML for low-code; custom training with containers, GPUs/TPUs.
  • Online (low-latency endpoint) vs batch prediction.
  • Feature Store for consistent feature serving.

MLOps

Vertex AI Pipelines automate the lifecycle; Model Monitoring detects drift and training/serving skew to trigger retraining.

🎯 Exam Tips

💡 Remember
  • Pick AutoML (low-code), custom training (control), or pretrained APIs (fastest).
  • Online prediction for real-time; batch prediction for large offline scoring.
  • Feature Store reduces training/serving skew.
  • Model Monitoring watches for drift and skew in production.
