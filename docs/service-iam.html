Core model

An IAM policy binds members (who) to roles (what) on a resource (where). Policies are inherited down the hierarchy; the effective policy is the union.

Roles

  • Basic - Owner/Editor/Viewer (avoid in prod).
  • Predefined - granular per service.
  • Custom - exact permissions you choose.

Service accounts

Identities for workloads. Prefer attaching SAs to resources and using Workload Identity Federation over downloading JSON keys.

🎯 Exam Tips

💡 Remember
  • Effective permissions are the UNION of all inherited policies.
  • Grant least privilege with predefined/custom roles, not basic roles.
  • Service Account User role lets a principal impersonate an SA.
  • Prefer Workload Identity Federation over long-lived SA keys.
